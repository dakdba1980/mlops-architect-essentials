🚨 **Why MLOps Fails Without Platform Engineering**

A lot of companies think MLOps = “add MLflow, Kubeflow, or SageMaker.”
But without a **solid platform foundation**, most MLOps efforts collapse. Here’s why:

1️⃣ **Data scientists ≠ infra engineers**
They need quick experiments + deployments. Without a self-service platform, every change depends on DevOps/SRE → delays + frustration.

2️⃣ **Security & compliance gaps**
ML pipelines handle sensitive data. If the underlying platform doesn’t enforce IAM, audit logging, and compliance as code → production is fragile.

3️⃣ **Scaling pain**
Running one model is easy. Running **hundreds** needs Kubernetes operators, feature stores, monitoring, and retraining loops. Without platform-level abstractions, it’s chaos.

✅ **Platform Engineering + MLOps = Success**
Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs) bring automation, self-service, and governance.
This creates **golden paths** for ML teams → faster delivery, consistent compliance, reliable scale.

👉 MLOps is not just a tool problem. It’s a **platform problem**.
